[ SHOCKWAVE PAD ]

Pair in iPad Settings → Bluetooth, then press any button — the PAD pill goes green. iOS hides gamepads from Safari until the first press.

  • D-PADMove — full 4-way, ship nudges vertically too
  • A B X YFire — any face button, hold for auto-fire
  • START ☰Start / Pause
  • RUMBLEFire — light kick · Kill — double-pulse · Boss kill — heavy thump
  • RUMBLEHit — heavy thump · Death — long rumble · Pickup — tick · Last ship — heartbeat

Haptics use the pad's vibrationActuator (dual-rumble) when present. Check the RUMBLE pill in the HUD — READY means an actuator was found, NO ACTUATOR means this pad reports no motors. Fallback: navigator.vibrate() on devices that support it (Android Chrome; not iPad / iOS Safari).
