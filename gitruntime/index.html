Different from another “explain my repo” tool.

GitRuntime focuses on the operational boundary of a codebase: what must exist around it before it can build, test, run, and deploy. The output is deterministic and source-linked, making it useful before cloning a repository, reviewing an unfamiliar project, or handing context to an agent.
