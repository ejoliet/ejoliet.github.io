Bitcoin 19 wallets · FBI list

Sent in 11 transactions, then peel-chained: 17 wallets of exactly 1,000 BTC, one of ~832.5 BTC, one of ~437.7 BTC. Tiny extra dust is spam sent by strangers. Balances below are live from mempool.space — funded minus spent.

Ethereum 4 key wallets · of 600+ FBI-listed

The FBI published 600+ ETH addresses; these four carry the story. Native ETH balance only, live via Blockscout — the main receiver's billions in USDT/USDC are frozen by Tether and Circle and don't show here.

Solana 1 wallet · FBI list

About 970,500 SOL (~$96M) was taken in total. This is the single Solana address the FBI published. Live balance via Solana RPC; if the public RPC refuses the browser request, the card says so and links out instead of guessing.

How it unfolded · UTC

  • OCT 28Staging. Operator wallet receives 0.1 ETH from Tornado Cash, deploys the sweeper contract the next day.
  • OCT 29 · 20:05First move. Five Binance wallets on the XRP Ledger send 233.46M XRP to one attacker address.
  • OCT 29 · 20:20Bitcoin. A Binance hot wallet sends 18,270 BTC in 11 transactions — the 19 wallets above, all in block 973,732.
  • OCT 29 · 20:24Ethereum. An EIP-7702 delegation turns Binance 14 into a sweeper: 167,464 ETH + 1.615B USDT gone in one call.
  • OCT 29–30Freezes. Tether, Circle, Ripple and others freeze ~$2.7–3.0B in stablecoins. BTC and most ETH can't be frozen.
  • OCT 31FBI list. FBI + USSS + IRS-CI name TraderTraitor and publish these BTC / ETH / SOL addresses, urging services to block them.

What this page can't see

  • Other chains: Tron (~612.7M USDT), XRP, Arbitrum, Avalanche, Polygon, Optimism and Base were also hit. The FBI published no address list for them.
  • New addresses: the moment a wallet spends, peel chains create fresh addresses not on this list. Chainalysis expects "tens of thousands" as laundering proceeds.
  • Token balances: ETH cards show native ETH only, not ERC-20 tokens.
  • It's a watch, not an alarm clock: checks run only while this page is open in your browser.
