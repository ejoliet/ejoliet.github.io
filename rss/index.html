Private sharing

Your library stays in this browser. Export an encrypted copy to give selected people your subscriptions or transfer them to another device. Sharing is manual, not live synchronization.

Send the file and the passphrase through separate channels. The file includes feed names, addresses, and cookie-use preferences, but never your cookies, credentials, saved articles, or reading history. A recipient still needs access to any restricted feed.
